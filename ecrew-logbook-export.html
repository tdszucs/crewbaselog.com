How to export your logbook from eCrew

eCrew keeps a record of every sector you fly in the Pilot LogBook report. Here is how to download it and turn it into a proper pilot logbook.

Export the Pilot LogBook report

  1. Log in to eCrew on a computer or phone.
  2. Open Reports in the menu and choose Pilot LogBook report.
  3. Set the Period and press Go. eCrew exports up to 2 years at a time: for a longer history, export each 2-year period in turn.
  4. Press Export (the save icon with a small arrow, next to the print icons) and choose CSV, Excel or PDF. All three work. On iPhone, save the file to Files.

Turn it into a logbook

  1. Open CrewBase Log and go to Your data.
  2. Drop the report, or several reports at once, into step 3. Overlapping periods are fine: flights already in your logbook are skipped.
  3. Download your logbook in step 4 as an EASA, UK CAA, South African CAA or FAA PDF, and keep the full backup for next time.

What is read from the report

Date, departure and arrival airport and time, aircraft type, registration, flight time, PIC name, take-offs and landings, PIC and co-pilot time, instructor time and simulator sessions. eCrew times are in UTC and stay in UTC.

The report has no night or IFR column, so CrewBase Log works out night time from the route and block times, and logs airline sectors as IFR. Simulator sessions (OPC, LPC, recurrent) go into the FSTD columns, and their landings are not counted as flight landings.

Next month

Restore your full backup, drop the new eCrew report and download the updated logbook. Flights that haven't departed yet are left out: the logbook only holds flights already flown.

Your roster too

The Personal Crew Schedule PDF from eCrew can go into Crew Base, where you and your friends see at a glance which days you are all off at base.

CrewBase Log is free. No subscription, no app to install: it runs in your browser on phone or computer, and your logbook stays on your device.
