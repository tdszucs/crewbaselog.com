Your data, your control

  • Your logbook, profile, signature and expiry dates are stored only in this browser on this device. We never see them.
  • Clearing your browser data (cookies and site data), or opening CrewBase Log on another phone or browser, starts empty: everything has to come back from your files.
  • The files you download are yours and stay on your device: the CSV file brings back all your flights; the full backup brings back everything, including profile and signature. Download them after each update.
  • Only a Crew Base roster you add is saved to your account, for the friends you choose.

Troubleshooting

Every line of your logbook is checked for the usual import mistakes. Edit, delete or mark each line as correct. A file read completely wrongly: undo the import, tick Let me match the columns myself under "Which files work?" in step 2 and drop it again.

3Import new flights from eCrew

The eCrew Pilot LogBook report. eCrew exports up to 2 years at a time: drop as many reports as you need, together or one after another. Flights already in your logbook are skipped.

How to get the report from eCrew
  1. Log in to eCrew and open Reports → Pilot LogBook report.
  2. Set the Period (up to 2 years) and press Go. For a longer history, export each 2-year period in turn.
  3. Press Export (the save icon with ▾) and choose CSV, Excel or PDF. On iPhone, save it to Files.
  4. Drop the files above, all at once if you like. Times are in UTC; overlapping periods are fine; simulator sessions go into the FSTD columns.

My roster

Drop your Personal Crew Schedule Report (PDF from AIMS) or click to choose it
Every day is read from it: duty and layovers, days off, standby and available. Times are kept in UTC; a roster printed in local base time is converted to UTC.
How to get the PDF
eCrew app (phone): More → Personal Crew Schedule → Create → choose the dates → Go. The PDF is in UTC.
eCrew website (computer): Crew Schedule → My Schedule → choose the period → Times in: UTC or Local Base → Print. A Local Base PDF is converted to UTC here; you confirm the time difference.
Crew Base keeps every roster in UTC so they compare on the same clock. Choose this roster's offset from UTC and its times are converted.
