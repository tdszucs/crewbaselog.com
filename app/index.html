My roster

Drop your Personal Crew Schedule Report (PDF from AIMS) or click to choose it
Every day is read from it: duty and layovers, days off, standby and available. Times are kept in UTC; a roster printed in local base time is converted to UTC.
How to get the PDF
eCrew app (phone): More → Personal Crew Schedule → Create → choose the dates → Go. The PDF is in UTC.
eCrew website (computer): Crew Schedule → My Schedule → choose the period → Times in: UTC or Local Base → Print. A Local Base PDF is converted to UTC here; you confirm the time difference.
Crew Base keeps every roster in UTC so they compare on the same clock. Choose this roster's offset from UTC and its times are converted.

Friends

Sharing rosters automatically needs a free account. Create one: your logbook comes along. Without one, send each other your roster files (Export to send to a friend, Load a roster a friend sent me).
Add each other by the email you log in with: once you have both added each other, your rosters appear here side by side and stay up to date. Your roster is saved to your account only for this; your logbook is never shared. A friend without an account can still send you their roster PDF to load.

How CrewBase Log works

  1. 1
    Drop your files

    Your past logbook from another app, your airline's eCrew / AIMS report and your roster: drop them all on Your data. CrewBase Log works out what each file is, puts every flight into one logbook and skips the ones already in. After a flight, add it in seconds with Add.

  2. 2
    Download your logbook

    A PDF logbook in the official layout (EASA, UK CAA, FAA or South African CAA), with your name and signature on every page, ready to print or show. Plus a CSV file and a full backup: your own copies, kept outside the app.

  3. 3
    Plan days off with colleagues

    Drop your future roster and add colleagues by their email. Crew Base puts your rosters side by side and shows the days you are all free at base, for a dinner, a trip or swapping a duty.

Your profile (name, licence and signature) goes on the cover and every page of the PDF. Check your profile

Your data, your control

  • Your logbook, profile, signature and expiry dates are saved in your account as you go, and on this device. Only your log-in can open them.
  • Log in on another phone or computer and your logbook is there. Clearing your browser data doesn't lose it.
  • The files you download are your own copy, outside the app: the CSV file brings back all your flights; the full backup brings back everything, including profile and signature. Download one after big changes.
  • A Crew Base roster you add is shared only with the friends you choose.

A file that doesn't work?

A logbook from another app, an eCrew or airline report or a roster that CrewBase Log can't read, or reads wrongly? Send us the file. Files sent in are checked several times a day and CrewBase Log is taught to read them, for free. Whether it works shows here after your next log-ins: then drop it again on Your data.

Delete your account

Your logbook, profile, Crew Base roster and account.

  • Your logbook is removed from this device at once and you are logged out.
  • Crew Base sharing stops at once: friends no longer see your roster.
  • The server keeps your logbook for 30 days. Log in before then and you can keep your account.
  • After 30 days your logbook, profile, roster and account are deleted for good. Nobody can bring them back.
  • Other phones or computers where you are logged in keep their own copy until you log out there.

Usage

Every pilot, with or without an account, counted anonymously: once a month per device (active), and each import, PDF, backup, restore and Crew Base roster. Pilots can switch this off in Profile.

Flights and device come from each pilot's latest app opening. Tap a pilot to see their activity.

Activity

Files sent for help

Files pilots sent because the app couldn't read them. Claude checks new ones every 8 hours, fixes the reader, marks them Works now and deletes them. Any file still here after 30 days is deleted automatically. A file also turns to Works now by itself when its pilot imports it successfully. Then press Email the pilot: your email app opens with the message ready.

Debug: wipe everything

Only on your account, for testing. Deletes every flight, your profile, signature, expiries, previous experience, import checks, the half-entered flight and your Crew Base roster and friends (on this device and on the server), then starts again with the setup, like a new pilot.

Help us read your file

Every logbook app and airline system writes its files a little differently. Send us this one and we'll teach CrewBase Log to read it. It's free, and every pilot using the same app or airline benefits.
