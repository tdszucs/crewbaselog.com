How to get your file from eCrew
  1. Log in to your airline's eCrew portal.
  2. In the left menu open Reports → Pilot LogBook report.
  3. Set the Period (for example 01/09/2026 – 30/09/2026, or a whole year) and press Go.
  4. When the report appears, click the Export button in the report toolbar (the save icon with a small ▾ arrow, next to the print icons).
  5. Choose CSV. If CSV isn't listed, choose Excel (XLSX); both work.
  6. Save the file, then drop it in the box above.

Tips: export month by month or a full year at a time. Flights you've already imported are skipped automatically, so overlapping periods are fine. The heading, crew line and "Totals" row in the file are ignored. Only actual flights are imported. Simulator sessions from the "Synth. devices" columns aren't imported yet.
