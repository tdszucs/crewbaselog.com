CrewBase Log

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

CrewBase Log is a pilot logbook. Your flying records are saved privately in your account, so they are safe and follow you to any device you log in on. This page explains what information we handle and why.

Your logbook

Your logbook (flights, previous experience, profile details such as name, licence number and type, home base and airline code, your signature, expiry dates, and the corrections and settings you make) is saved in your account with our database provider, Supabase, as you work. It is protected by your log-in: only your account can read or change it. It is not shared with anyone and is never used for advertising. The site owner can technically access the database to run and repair the service, but does not look at pilots' logbooks.

A working copy is also kept in your browser on each device you use, so the app is fast and works offline; changes made offline are saved to your account when the connection is back.

Files you import (logbook exports, PDFs, eCrew reports and roster PDFs) are read inside your browser. The files themselves are not uploaded; only the flights and details read from them become part of your logbook. The CSV file and full backup you download are your own copies, outside the app.

To open quickly and work offline, the app's own files and the libraries it uses are kept in your browser's cache. This cache contains no logbook data.

Crew Base rosters you choose to share

If you add a roster in Crew Base, the days from it (duty, standby and days off, with times and layover airports), the name and base you show to friends, and the email addresses of the friends you add are saved to your account. Your roster can be seen only by you and by the people whose email you have added as a friend. Your logbook is never part of this. Clearing your roster in Crew Base empties the saved copy; removing a friend stops them seeing it.

What we store to run your account

To let you log in we store your email address, an encrypted password (if you sign up with email) and basic sign-in records such as when your account was created and last used. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We use this only to identify your account.

How the app is used

When you are logged in, the app records which actions you take, so we can see how CrewBase Log is used and fix what doesn't work. For example: that you opened the app, imported two files (PDF and CSV) that added 152 lines, downloaded an EASA PDF logbook, fixed a line in Troubleshooting or saved a Crew Base roster. Each record holds your account, the time, the action and counts or formats such as the number of flights. It never holds your flights, the names or contents of your files, your profile details or your signature. Only the site owner can see these records.

Your logbook, account data, these usage records and shared Crew Base rosters are stored with our database and authentication provider, Supabase.

Anonymous usage counts

So we know how many pilots use CrewBase Log, the app sends anonymous counts, whether or not you have an account: once a month that it was used on a device, and a count each time a logbook is started, a file is imported, a PDF logbook or backup is downloaded, a backup is restored or a Crew Base roster is saved. Each count can carry your main aircraft family (for example "Airbus A320 family"), your licence type (ATPL, CPL…), a range of total hours (for example "1,500-5,000") and your device's time zone. It never contains your name, email, licence number, logbook, files or any identifier: nothing links two counts to the same pilot. Only the site owner can see the totals. You can switch this off in Profile, under How you log your flights.

Files you send us

If a file can't be read, the app offers to send it to us so we can add support for it. This happens only when you choose to send it and tick the box to agree. The file, the note you add, what the app reported and your email (your account email, or the one you type in if you have no account) are stored privately with Supabase. Only the site owner can open them, and they are used only to make CrewBase Log read that kind of file. We never share them. We delete the file once it can be read, and at the latest after 30 days. You can ask us to delete it sooner at hello@crewbaselog.com. Without an account, your device keeps a private code for each file you sent, so the app can show you its status; we use your email only to tell you when the file works. We also get an email through FormSubmit saying a file arrived, with its name and your note, but not the file itself.

Feedback

If you send feedback from the app, your message, the reply email you give and the email of your account are delivered to us by email through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-to-email service. We use them only to read and answer your feedback.

Services the app loads

To work, the app loads fonts from Google Fonts and software libraries (for sign-in, reading PDF and Excel files and making PDFs) from public content delivery networks (cdnjs, jsDelivr). Like any website, these services receive your IP address when they are loaded. They do not receive your logbook.

Support payments

If you choose to support CrewBase Log, the payment form you open on our site is provided by Ko-fi (ko-fi.com) and is loaded only when you open it. Your payment is made with Ko-fi and its payment providers (Stripe or PayPal) under their own privacy policies; we never see your card details. Ko-fi tells us the name and message you give with your support.

Emails

We send emails needed for your account, such as verifying your address or resetting your password. We do not send marketing emails unless you ask to receive them.

Job listings

CrewBase Log shows job listings from aviation employers. Viewing listings does not share your logbook or profile details with any advertiser.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it with third parties, except the service providers needed to run CrewBase Log (hosting, accounts, feedback delivery), or where the law requires it.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the account data we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account and your logbook at any time by emailing hello@crewbaselog.com. You can download your whole logbook from the app at any time (CSV file or full backup).

Google Calendar (roster). If you choose "Connect Google Calendar" on the Home page, CrewBase Log asks Google for read-only access to your calendars. It reads only the events created by the AIMS eCrew app (your duties, days off and standby) from two weeks back to four months ahead, and keeps them as your roster in your account, so you can see it next to your friends' rosters; your friends see your roster only if you add each other. No other calendar event is read, stored or shared, and the access token is used once and not kept. You can remove CrewBase Log's access at any time in your Google Account under Security → Third-party access, and delete your roster in the app. CrewBase Log's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: the data is used only to show your roster, is never sold, and is never used for advertising.

Looking for a job. On the Jobs page you can switch on "Looking for a job?" and choose what CrewBase Log may share to put you forward for vacancies: your profile, your email address, an hours summary, your complete logbook and a CV you upload. Nothing is shared while it is off, and nobody connected with CrewBase Log will contact you about jobs unless it is on. What you share is seen only by the CrewBase Log team, is never published or sold, and is not sent to an airline or crewing agency without asking you first. Unticking an item stops sharing it at once; switching it off deletes everything you shared there, including your CV, from our server.

You can also delete your account yourself in the app (More → Help → Delete your account). Your logbook leaves that device at once and your Crew Base roster stops being shared. We keep your logbook for 30 days in case you change your mind (logging in again within that time lets you keep your account), then your account, logbook, roster, activity and any files you sent us are deleted for good.
