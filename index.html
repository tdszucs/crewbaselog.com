Questions pilots ask

Is CrewBase Log free?

Yes. CrewBase Log is free for pilots, with no subscription and nothing locked. It is supported by airlines posting pilot jobs and by pilots who choose to buy us a coffee.

Where is my logbook stored?

On your own device, in your browser. Your flights, profile and signature are not sent to our servers. Download the full backup after each update to keep a copy and to move to a new phone or browser.

Which logbook formats can I print?

An EASA Part-FCL logbook (FCL.050), UK CAA, South African CAA (SACAA) and FAA (14 CFR 61.51) as a PDF: the full logbook, the last 10 pages or any dates, with page totals and your signature.

Can I import my logbook from Flylog, CrewLounge, LogTen or Safelog?

Yes. Flylog CSV and PDF, CrewLounge PILOTLOG (mccPILOTLOG) CSV and PDF, LogTen PDF logbooks and Safelog CSV are read automatically, as are EASA-style PDF logbooks. Any other CSV or Excel file works after you match its columns once.

How do I add flights from eCrew or AIMS?

Export the Pilot LogBook report from eCrew (Reports, Pilot LogBook report, Export as CSV, Excel or PDF) and drop it into CrewBase Log. Several 2-year reports can go in at once; duplicates are skipped and night time is worked out for you.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. CrewBase Log runs in the browser on phones, tablets and computers, and can be added to your Home Screen like an app.
