How it works

Set up your profile once (name, licence, signature), then every update takes a few minutes.

Add your files

Your old logbook (CSV, Excel or an EASA-style PDF) and the flights downloaded from AIMS / eCrew.

CrewBase Log merges them

Duplicates are skipped, night and IFR time filled in. It all happens in your browser.

Download your logbook

A PDF in EASA, UK CAA or FAA format, and a CSV of your full logbook to keep. Next time, start from that CSV.
