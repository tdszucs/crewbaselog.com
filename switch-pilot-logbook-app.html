Move your logbook from another app

Years of flying shouldn't be stuck in one app. Export them once and CrewBase Log carries your totals over, line by line.

Where to find the file in each app

LogTen Pro (Mac, iPhone, iPad)

  1. Open Reports, choose Exporters, then Export Flights (Tab). Not Dynamic Export: it leaves out the fields your logbook view hides.
  2. On iPhone or iPad, under Configure Report set Entry Type and Filter Group to All and the date range to All, then tap Generate and save or share the file. On a Mac, choose Save As.
  3. Drop the .txt file into step 2 on Your data. The EASA or SACAA logbook PDF from LogTen works too.

CrewLounge PILOTLOG (mccPILOTLOG)

  1. Check that PILOTLOG records your times in UTC.
  2. Open your logbook, tap Query, leave the date range empty to take the whole logbook, and choose Export Data, then CSV.
  3. Drop the CSV into step 2. The logbook PDF works too. A database backup (.mdb or JSON) can't be read.

Safelog

  1. Use Safelog's export to save your flights as a CSV file, not a Safelog backup.
  2. Drop the CSV into step 2. Day and night, single and multi-engine columns are added up for you.

Wader Pilot Logbook

  1. Export your logbook from Wader as the EASA logbook PDF, the one with Date, Departure, Arrival … on the left page.
  2. Drop the PDF into step 2. Its totals are checked against the PDF's own "Total time" rows.

Flylog

  1. In Flylog's sync settings connect Dropbox and choose the CSV export. The file, exportCSV.csv, appears in your Dropbox.
  2. Drop it into step 2. The EASA or JAR logbook PDF from Flylog works too.

Another app? Export your flights as CSV or Excel, or the logbook as a PDF in the EASA layout. If CrewBase Log can't read it, press Help us read it and we'll make it work, for free.

How the move works

  1. Enter hours you won't import flight by flight, such as an old paper logbook, as previous experience.
  2. Drop your export into step 2 on Your data. Simulator sessions, PIC, co-pilot, night and IFR time are kept as you logged them.
  3. Open Check your totals and type in the totals from the last page of your old logbook. Each column shows whether it matches.
  4. Troubleshooting lists lines that look wrong (totals entered as a flight, simulator sessions logged as flights, missing aircraft types, registrations without a dash) and lets you fix each one, with an undo.

After the move

Add new flights from your airline's eCrew report, download your logbook as an EASA, UK CAA, SACAA or FAA PDF, and keep a full backup. Your logbook is stored on your device, not on our servers, so you are never locked in: the CSV export opens in any spreadsheet.

CrewBase Log is free. No subscription, no app to install: it runs in your browser on phone or computer, and your logbook stays on your device.
